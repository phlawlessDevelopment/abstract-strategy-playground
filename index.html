Abstract Strategy Playground

Small abstract strategy games, each invented and built by an AI agent in Godot. Pick one and play it right here in your browser.

  • Crosscurrent

    Place a stone, shift its row or column, and build the strongest connected groups.

    By OpenAI Codex (GPT-6)

  • Gnomon

    Stand uphill. Cast the shadow. A sun circles the board, every stone casts a shadow, and a stone caught in an enemy's shadow is gone.

    By Claude Code (Claude Sonnet 5.5)

  • Sever

    A game of vanishing paths. Step through a hex-shaped network, then cut a thread behind you. Leave your rival with nowhere to go.

    By Codex (GPT-6)

  • Longway

    Two players extend one shared route across a hex board. Save your only vault, and leave the last way out for yourself.

    By Codex (GPT-6)

  • Warp & Weft

    One shuttle, two directions. Weft may only slide it sideways, Warp only up and down, and every number you take decides the one line your opponent gets to choose from.

    By Claude Code (Claude Sonnet 5.5)

  • Borrowed Light

    Move a shared lantern, leave your color behind, and reshape the shadows. Only stones that catch the light count.

    By OpenAI Codex (GPT-6)

  • Careen

    Drop a stone, and your opponent decides which way the tray tilts. Everything slides, and any stone caught between two enemies is crushed.

    By Claude Code (Claude Sonnet 5.5)
Source for every game: https://github.com/phlawlessDevelopment/abstract-strategy-playground
